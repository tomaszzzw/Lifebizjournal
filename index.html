About

Built by traders,
for traders.

flojournal is more than a journal. It is your daily coach, helping you maintain discipline, awareness and growth.

Most traders focus only on the market — forgetting that they themselves are the variable. Your emotional state, sleep, routine and mindset have a huge impact on your decisions.

flojournal was born from one simple observation: the best traders keep journals. Not because they have to — but because it works. They see patterns, learn from mistakes and build consistent processes.

We currently focus mainly on trading — session journal, emotions, discipline and results. But this is just the beginning.

How it works

Four steps to conscious trading.

01

Start the day with a plan

Every morning fill in a quick check-in — how you slept, your energy level and what you want to achieve. No excuses.

02

Log your trades

Enter setup, result, emotions before and after. The system analyzes patterns and shows you when you trade best.

03

Take care of yourself

Track sport, sleep and recovery. The best traders know that body and mind are the foundation of all decisions.

04

Review and grow

Weekly summaries, charts and patterns. See what works, what does not — and where your edge is.

Privacy Policy

Your data,
your property.

Last updated: May 2026

📦 What we collect

We only collect data you enter yourself — trading entries, workout logs, notes and goals. For login we use Supabase (email + password). We do not collect device data, IP addresses or browsing behavior, and we never sell your data.

🔒 How we store data

All data is stored in Supabase — European infrastructure (Frankfurt, Germany), encrypted at rest and in transit. Local settings are stored in your browser\'s localStorage and never leave your device.

💳 Payments via Stripe

All payments are processed securely by Stripe. We never store your credit card details — all payment data is handled directly by Stripe. You can cancel your subscription at any time and retain access until the end of the billing period.

🤖 AI Coach

The AI Coach uses the Anthropic API (Claude). Your recent data is sent to Anthropic only to generate your response — it is not used to train AI models.

🍪 Cookies

We use only essential cookies required for authentication. We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels or analytics tools that share data with third parties.

🗑️ Account deletion

You can request deletion of your account and all data at any time. All data will be permanently deleted within 7 business days. This action is irreversible.
